### Primary: Chip Security Act — Amendment in the Nature of a Substitute to H.R. 3447 (House text)
- **URL:** https://docs.house.gov/meetings/FA/FA00/20260326/118999/BILLS-1193447ANSih-U2.pdf

  > **Rules of construction — nothing may be construed to direct Commerce to:** “require any chip security mechanisms that may hinder the capability or functionality… such as a **kill switch or geofencing mechanism**, or meaningfully undermine the cybersecurity…”

### Primary (supporting): NVIDIA attestation docs exist as integrity tooling (not export kill switch)
- Example: https://docs.nvidia.com/attestation/quick-start-guide/latest/attestation-examples/blackwell_multi_gpu.html
